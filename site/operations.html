Operations

Observe

INFO and INFO prometheus describe the shard that received the command. When metrics_address is set, that shard also serves GET /metrics on a separate port. Successful GET, SET, and INCR only move counters. Histograms are scraped, not pushed per command.

Operational lines go to a bounded background writer. A full queue drops the line and increments ruvio_log_lines_dropped_total instead of stalling the shard. RUST_LOG selects the filter at startup.

Memory and expiry

max_shard_bytes and max_process_bytes default to 0, which disables those budgets. noeviction rejects writes over the budget. Sampled policies are allkeys-lru, allkeys-lfu, volatile-lru, and volatile-ttl. Deadlines are Unix milliseconds. After recovery the clock floor is max(CLOCK, checkpoint.created_at).
